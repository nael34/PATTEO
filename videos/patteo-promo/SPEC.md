# Creative spec — PATTEO, le rituel du retour

- **One thing:** a chenille paw mat for the small pause at the end of a walk.
- **Viewer:** a French dog owner arriving home after a walk.
- **Story spine:** walk ends → entryway pause → texture and honest benefit →
  three colour choices → starting price and delivery → shop CTA.
- **Format:** 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 9:16, approximately 30 seconds, French.
- **LOOK:** ground `#faf8f2`; ink `#173e50`; soft ground `#fffefa`; blue
  `#dfebee`; yellow `#f1d578`; sage `#e3e8d9`; bundled Inter, 700/500;
  large editorial type with generous margins; product photography in its
  original proportions where possible.
- **SOUND:** warm French Gemini narration; no music bed in this cut; a single
  short whoosh for the tactile reveal, a light whoosh on the colour change, and
  a soft pop as the price lands. Effects stay beneath the voice.
- **BANNED by default:** invented mud-cleaning demo, fake customer proof, logo
  docked in a corner, three-card feature list, repeated fade-and-slide entrances,
  chimes, dense particle effects, or dimensions baked into product photography.

## Shot plan

1. **0–4.5s — Hook:** real PATTEO lifestyle image, dog and mat in the entry;
   “Après la balade” opens the story. The first frame is a full-bleed product
   world, never a logo or black screen.
2. **4.2–8.7s — Texture:** macro chenille, tactile push-in; “Une pause à
   l’entrée”.
3. **8.4–13.6s — Benefit:** mat by the door; communicate the on-site claim,
   “aide à retenir l’humidité et les saletés au contact”.
4. **13.3–19.4s — Choice:** a brisk, individual reveal of gris galet, bleu
   balade and jaune soleil, each shown on its own full frame.
5. **19.1–25.0s — Offer:** return to the product; “Du XS au XL”, “dès
   23,90 €”, “livraison comprise”.
6. **24.7–30s — End card:** real logo arrives with a purposeful wipe; hold
   “Le retour tout doux” and “Choisis ton coloris” for at least two seconds.

The visual beats are not subtitles: they support but do not repeat the spoken
sentences word for word. Transition overlaps are intentional crossfades; the
camera's direction changes between scenes so the film does not feel like a
template.
