# PATTEO — the return-home ritual

Make one finished, vertical social ad of about 30 seconds. No campaign is to be
created or launched. Use the existing product photography and logo in
`assets/photos/`. Those website visuals are identified as AI-created staging
imagery; do not imply that they are customer photos or documentary footage.

The actual product is a chenille paw mat. The website says its chenille texture
helps retain moisture and dirt on contact with paws. It comes in grey, blue and
yellow. Size pricing ranges from €23.90 (XS) to €29.90 (XL), with delivery
included. Keep the ad concise: “du XS au XL”, “dès 23,90 €” and “livraison
comprise” are sufficient; do not print dimensions over the product images.

Use the brand palette sampled from the live site: deep teal `#173e50`, warm
cream `#faf8f2`, soft white `#fffefa`, muted blue `#dfebee`, warm yellow
`#f1d578`, and sage `#e3e8d9`. Use bundled Inter with a restrained editorial
display treatment. Keep titles within mobile safe areas and the logo for the
final card. The CTA should read “Choisis ton coloris” and point viewers to the
PATTEO shop without inventing a discount or urgency.

Use a French Gemini narration with a warm, conversational commercial read. Keep
music off for this version; use a few quiet, licensed bundled effects, mixed
under the narration. Avoid chimes, fake mud-removal demonstrations, fake
testimonials, and unsupported performance promises.
