# Storylines considered

## Recommended — Le rituel du retour

One familiar moment: a walk ends, a dog comes home, and PATTEO takes its place
by the door. A close-up earns the chenille claim; the colour reveal and honest
price turn the small domestic ritual into a clear invitation. Gentle editorial
photography, varied camera moves, restrained whooshes and a warm French voice.

## Alternative — Trois couleurs, trois maisons

Three quick colour-led worlds move from grey to blue to yellow, then converge on
the same product and price. More graphic and faster, but less emotionally
memorable than following one return-home moment.

## Alternative — Le détail de la chenille

An intimate macro journey through the textile, pulling back to reveal the mat
at the door and then the dog. Tactile and product-focused, but the site's
existing lifestyle image makes the recommended story warmer and easier to
understand at a glance.
