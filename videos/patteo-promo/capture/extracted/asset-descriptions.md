# PATTEO asset inventory

These are existing assets from `dist/assets/` in the PATTEO website repository.
No new product image or customer photo was invented for this ad.

- `assets/photos/logo.png` — official PATTEO wordmark and paw mark on transparent background.
- `assets/photos/hero.webp` — warm home-entry lifestyle composition, dog beside the grey chenille mat; site labels this visual as AI-created staging imagery.
- `assets/photos/patteo-chien.jpg` — dog resting on the mat; illustrative staging image.
- `assets/photos/texture-macro.jpg` — close view of the chenille fibres; illustrative close-up.
- `assets/photos/patteo-gris-porte.jpg` — grey mat at a doorway; illustrative staging image.
- `assets/photos/patteo-gris-detail.jpg` — overhead detail of the grey mat in an entryway; illustrative staging image.
- `assets/photos/gris.webp` — grey product cutout.
- `assets/photos/bleu.webp` — blue product cutout.
- `assets/photos/jaune.webp` — yellow product cutout.

Brand colours are from the site's CSS. Fonts: the site declares PatteoSans with
local Avenir Next / Avenir / Segoe UI fallbacks and Georgia for serif display;
this video uses bundled Inter because those local fallbacks are not shipped with
the project.
